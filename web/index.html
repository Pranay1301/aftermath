Safer can mean unfinished.

Across this fixed matrix, contract-aware recovery completed 18 of 21 cases with one effect, duplicated none and held 3 uncertain outcomes. It does not complete every task.

In two held cases nothing happened at the provider. Without an outcome query, the worker cannot safely assume that.

These are deterministic local fixtures, not an LLM benchmark, a production guarantee or a claim of universal exactly-once execution. Local file locks are not distributed locks. Provider lookup is assumed strongly consistent. Six chosen cuts are not exhaustive crash exploration.
